2AUSPLAY Running Participation Rates by State and Age (2025)

Key insights

  • Participation drops steadily across older age groups.
  • Victoria has the top youth rate: 31.9%.
  • Northern Territory peaks at ages 25–34: 31.3%.
  • NSW/ACT peaks at ages 18–24: 30.45%.
  • Seniors aged 65+ fall below 3%.

3Running/Jogging Participation Rate: Female vs Male by State

Key insights

  • Males lead females in almost every state.
  • ACT records the highest rates for both genders.
  • SA has the lowest female rate: 3.5%.
  • WA is the only state with equal rates.
  • Overall participation averages roughly 5.2% across groups.

Part 3

The health context

How charity run activity sits alongside chronic disease and health burden.

Line & bar chart

9Charity Runs Registered vs Selected Causes of Death (2023–2024)

Key insights

  • Deaths from all three listed causes rose in 2024.
  • Chronic lower respiratory disease was the leading cause.
  • New charity registrations fell nearly 14% from 2023 to 2024.
  • An inverse trend appears: registrations fell while deaths rose.

Two series moving in opposite directions show association, not causation.

Heat map

10Long-Term Health Condition Prevalence by Australian State

Key insights

  • Mental conditions are most prevalent nationwide: 26.1%.
  • Queensland has the highest mental condition rate: 30.8%.
  • Tasmania records the highest rates of multiple conditions.
  • NT shows the lowest arthritis prevalence: 11.2%.
  • Cancer, COPD and kidney disease are least prevalent.

Tree map

11Types of Diseases in Australia (2018)

Key insights

  • Cancer and neoplasms carry the highest group burden.
  • Cardiovascular diseases exceed 550,000 DALYs.
  • Coronary heart disease is the leading single condition.
  • Back pain records a high burden: 222,357 DALYs.
  • Oral disorders form the smallest disease group.

DALY: disability-adjusted life year, a measure of disease burden.
